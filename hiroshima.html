WONDER NAN & GRAND BAT VISIT HIROSHIMA

A CITY OF PEACE & RESILIENCE — HOME TO JAPAN'S MOST MOVING MEMORIAL AND THE FLOATING TORII OF MIYAJIMA

WHERE THEY ARE

[ The ship docks right in Hiroshima for the day. The Peace Memorial Park is a short tram or taxi ride from the pier, and the ferry to Miyajima Island leaves from a nearby terminal. ]

PORT: HIROSHIMA TO PEACE PARK: [ -- ] MINS STAY: ONE DAY

WHAT THEY'LL SEE

PEACE MEMORIAL PARK & ATOMIC BOMB DOME
ITSUKUSHIMA SHRINE'S FLOATING TORII
HIROSHIMA CASTLE
SHUKKEIEN GARDEN

The Peace Memorial Park and Museum mark the 1945 atomic bombing and honour its victims — a quiet, reflective stop rather than a sightseeing highlight. Most visitors find it well worth the time.

WHAT THEY MIGHT DO

Walk through the Peace Memorial Park
Take the ferry across to Miyajima Island
Ride the cable car up Mt. Misen
Stroll Shukkeien's miniature landscape garden
Climb Hiroshima Castle's keep
Browse Okonomimura's okonomiyaki stalls
