BAT-COMPUTER CITY FILE: KYOTO 京都

OPEN THE KYOTO FILE!
HISTORY FILE

Japan's imperial capital for over 1,000 years (794–1868), until the emperor moved to Tokyo. Spared from WWII bombing, so it still holds 1,600+ Buddhist temples and 400+ Shinto shrines.

MUST-SEE SIGHTS

Fushimi Inari's torii gate tunnels, Kinkaku-ji's Golden Pavilion, Arashiyama's bamboo grove, Gion's lantern-lit streets, Nijo Castle, Kiyomizu-dera.

FOOD TO TRY

Kaiseki (multi-course dining), yudofu (simmered tofu), kyo-wagashi sweets with matcha, and the food stalls of Nishiki Market.

LOCAL CUSTOMS

Bow rather than shake hands, remove shoes indoors, don't eat while walking, keep calls and chat quiet on trains, and there's no tipping.
